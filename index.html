Prayers for Catholics

About this app

A companion for everyday Catholic prayer. It holds a growing collection of prayers and, once installed, works without an internet connection.

How it was made

This app was built by Claude, an AI model created by Anthropic, using material provided to it and under the guidance of Keith Francis (GitHub: keithintrinidad).

The collection began with the Prayers for Catholics leaflet of the Parish of the Assumption, supplied by Keith Francis. All of its prayers are included, and further traditional prayers have been added at his direction, with more to come. He directed the app’s content, features and packaging through conversation with Claude, but did not write the code directly.

It is a companion to Rosary Helper (repo) and Bread of the Presence (repo), made the same way.
